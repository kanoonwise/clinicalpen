        Questions

        Asked by students and doctors before they start.

        I have three months left and no analysis. Can you help?

        Usually yes, if your data is collected. We clean the master chart, run the analysis in about a week and then edit chapters as you write them. If the data does not exist we cannot create it, and we will tell you what to discuss with your guide.

        Will my guide know I used Clinical Pen?

        We encourage you to tell them. Statistical help and language editing are normal and should be acknowledged in your thesis or paper. Every piece of work goes back to your guide for approval.

        Do you help with MBBS assignments and case records?

        Yes. We give feedback on structure, references and language in what you wrote, and teach you how to answer well. We do not write assignments for you to submit.

        I am a practising doctor, not a student. Can you help?

        Yes. We support doctors and faculty with papers, reviews, book chapters, grant proposals and statistics, under the same rules: the work and the authorship stay yours.

        Do you guarantee my paper will be published?

        No. Nobody honest can. We improve the manuscript, choose a suitable indexed journal and help you answer reviewers.

        Which universities and boards do you cover?

        State health universities, deemed universities and NBEMS programmes. Formats and deadlines differ, so we work from your own university's current guidelines.

        Is my patient data safe?

        Send only de-identified data. We sign a confidentiality agreement, limit access to your assigned statistician and delete files after the project on request.
